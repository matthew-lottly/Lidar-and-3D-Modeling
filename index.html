Rooftop solar potential, West University Place and downtown Houston

Roofs found by shape, split into planes by RANSAC, shaded by neighbours and trees, summed over an NREL typical year; a notebook does it for any box and writes .glb and .obj.

  • 104 / 30buildings, West U / downtown
  • 23% / 55%direct sun lost to shade
  • 1,198 kWh/m2mean on West U roofs per year

Checked against: Overture footprints and heights; clear-sky upper bound. Partly validated. No measured roof irradiance or PV output; the height disagreement with Overture is unexplained and is not claimed either way. Details

Limit: Roofs under trees are missing; totals move about 10% with the shading surface.

Method, numbers and limits

Topographic differencing, Pacifica bluffs, 2010 to 2023

Three surveys registered on stable ground (ICP), DEMs of difference with a slope-dependent level of detection, M3C2 on the face, cut-and-fill volumes and contour retreat.

  • -23,362 m3net, 2016 to 2023, coastal band
  • -2,686 m3net between two surveys with no change (null test)
  • 0.8 mmedian retreat of the 12 m contour, 2016 to 2023

Checked against: Null test with two independent 2010 surveys; level of detection recalibrated on it. Validated by null test. The 2010-2016 change is not claimed; the 2016-2023 loss is. No ground survey (GNSS, profiles) was compared. Details

Limit: No field survey. The 2010 to 2016 change is inside the null-test noise and is not claimed.

Floods

Drainage and flood-stage road vulnerability, Norfolk, Virginia

A 1 m bare-earth terrain conditioned with pysheds, culvert-obstruction candidates at road embankments, and bathtub flooding at stages from 1.5 to 4 m against the drivable road network.

  • 6.5 cmterrain hold-out error
  • 39 of 51 kmroads under 30 cm of water at a 3 m stage
  • 24 to 53 haflooded at 2.5 m if the stage is off by 10 cm

Checked against: Flight-line repeatability; USGS DTM; Chesapeake 1 m land cover; JRC water; Overture bridges. Partly validated. Heights and road classes are checked; flood extent, depth and the tide link are not (no gauge, benchmark or observed flood data). Details

Limit: Static bathtub: no rain, drains, pumps, tide or storm.

Method, numbers and limits

Accuracy. Each analysis is checked against independent real data where it exists (a second survey, overlapping flight lines, satellite maps, a null test); the full table and what is still unchecked is in docs/accuracy-assessment.md.

How to read this. The plates are the results; each card links to the write-up with the method, the error tests and what was not done. Where something could not be verified (no field data, no survey checkpoints, no repeat lidar) the write-up says so. Interactive files: Tatoosh point cloud, Seattle point cloud, White River utility scene, Houston canopy map, Tahoe canopy map, and the rooftop solar notebook. Full detail: README.
